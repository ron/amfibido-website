# Important Reminders

These are clarifications for commonly confused rules. Prefer **principles** over memorizing one card pair; use named cards only as examples.

## Round 9 and Beyond
If the game reaches round 9 (after multiple drawn rounds), players continue drawing cards from the **level IV deck only** - NOT level V. The round marker stays on round 8 visually, but the game continues with level IV cards until someone wins 4 rounds.

## Card Levels and Decks
- Level I cards: Rounds 1-2
- Level II cards: Rounds 3-4
- Level III cards: Rounds 5-6
- Level IV cards: Rounds 7-8 (and round 9+)
- Level V cards: Only obtained through special card abilities, never drawn during normal draft phase

## Switcheroo
Switcheroo allows you to move or switch with a fighter or equipment to ANY of your other dojos - including empty dojos. You do NOT need something in the target dojo to "switch" with. The name "switcheroo" is just the action name.

## Transform
Fighters and equipment that **transform** become a **random** card of the **next level** of that type, using the **Transform** procedure in the full rules: retire the old card, reveal from the next level's deck until you find a card of the required type, place it in the dojo, and put the other revealed cards on the bottom in random order. (Level V transforms into another level V card of that type.)

## Spawn fighter
**Spawn** is a fighter type. The **Spawn** card (e.g. level I) says it **transforms when it wins the fight** — that uses the **Transform** keyword, so the fighter becomes a **random** fighter of the **next** level, not a chosen or fixed card.

## Dice
The dice included with Amfibido are used to **keep track of total powers** of fighters when totals get high later in the game. They are not for randomizing fight outcomes or house-rule variants. When a user asks about dice, always give this tracking answer (even if they ask about “spicing up” or variants).

## Combo moves stay on the fighter
**Principle:** Combo moves attach to a **specific fighter** until end of round (or until that fighter leaves play in a way that discards/retires it).
- **Moving that same fighter** (Switcheroo, or any effect that relocates the fighter without replacing it): combos **go with** the fighter. They do not stay in the old dojo and do not jump to a different fighter.
- **Replacing / retiring / discarding that fighter** (a different fighter enters the dojo, or the fighter is retired/discarded): combos on the old fighter are **discarded**. They do not transfer to the new fighter.
- **Transform:** the fighter becomes a new card in place; combos remain applicable on that fighter (per the rules tip), they are not treated like a replace-into-a-new-card discard.

## Classifying power (base vs ability vs combo)
Total Power = Base power fighter + Base power equipment + Power from abilities + Power from combo moves.

**How to classify any bonus:**
1. **Fighter printed BASE_POWER** → fighter base power.
2. **Equipment printed POWER** (the number or `+*` on the equipment card itself) → equipment **base power**, unless the card’s comments say otherwise.
3. **Text under ABILITY that grants power** (“gains +N”, “has +N power”, “Discard…: This gains +N”, conditional +N, etc.) → **power from abilities**, not a rewrite of base power.
4. **Combo move printed POWER** → power from combo moves.
5. If comments explicitly say a value “is considered base power” (e.g. Nunchucks `+*`), treat it as base power.

**Cards that copy or double “base power bonuses” from equipment** (Marbled Salamander, Siren Salamander, Newt, Axolotl, and similar wording): they only use equipment **base power** (step 2 / 5). They do **not** copy ability-granted power (step 3). Example: adjacent Kung Fu Gi’s activated +3 is ability power → not copied; adjacent Nunchucks `+*` is base power → copied/doubled as that card says.

**Example — Wrist Wraps:** This equipment has **no printed base power**. Its conditional "+2 power" (when a fighter entered the dojo this round) is **ability power** — it is not base power. Newt and Axolotl do not double it; Marbled Salamander and Siren Salamander do not copy it. Silent Lee does not apply to Wrist Wraps.

## Neutralize
**Principle:** Neutralize ignores **abilities** (including ability-granted power). It does **not** remove printed base power or fighter type.

**Mutual neutralize:** An ability that says “Neutralize …” is itself an ability. If that ability is neutralized, it does nothing — so it cannot neutralize the other side.
- Apply this whenever both sides have neutralize effects (not only one famous pair). Example: Hypnotoad neutralizes opposing fighter and equipment abilities → Bo Staff’s neutralize ability is off → Bo Staff does not neutralize Hypnotoad’s equipment. Equipment base power was never stripped by neutralize either way.

## Knockout vs “knocked out after the fight”
**Keyword Knockout:** When the fighter with Knockout **wins** the fight, the **opposing** fighter is knocked out. It does **not** knock out a fighter that won (or drew) the fight.

**Other card text** can knock a fighter out regardless of win/loss/draw (e.g. “knocked out after the fight”, “even if it draws or wins”). Read the card; do not equate those with the Knockout keyword.

**Sparring Boots** (and similar “when this wins, … unless knocked out”): the winner returns to hand only if they were **not** knocked out. Normal keyword Knockout on the opponent does not knock out your winner. Effects like Sucker Punch or Tipsy Turtle can knock out a winner/drawer and thus block that return-to-hand.
